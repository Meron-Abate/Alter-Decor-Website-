About Us

Alter Spaces was founded on the belief that decor is not decoration — it is atmosphere. For a decade we have shaped rooms, tents, gardens, and ballrooms into worlds that carry the weight of the occasion. Our studio brings together florists, set designers, and lighting artists who treat each event as a singular composition.

Every space holds a memory before it's made.
